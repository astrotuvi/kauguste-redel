Hubble'i seadusThe Hubble law

Taavi Tuvikene
TÜ Tartu observatooriumTartu Observatory, University of Tartu

Mida kaugemal on galaktika, seda kiiremini ta meist eemaldub. Kiirus on kaugusega võrdeline, v = H0 d, ja võrdetegur H0 on Hubble'i konstant. Kiirust on lihtne mõõta: eemalduva galaktika valgus on nihkunud pikemate lainepikkuste poole, ja see punanihe on spektris otse näha. Kaugust on palju raskem mõõta, ja selleks ongi vaja kauguste redelit. Kui H0 on kord teada, saab seadust kasutada ka teistpidi: punanihkest saab iga galaktika kauguse. H0 määrab ka Universumi mõõtkava: kui kaugel on galaktikad ja kui vana on Universum. Seose leidsid Georges Lemaître (1927) ja Edwin Hubble (1929); Rahvusvaheline Astronoomiaunioon soovitab alates 2018. aastast nimetada seda Hubble'i–Lemaître'i seaduseks. Harjutuse 3. ja 4. osas mõõdad H0 ise.

The farther away a galaxy is, the faster it recedes from us. The velocity is proportional to the distance, v = H0 d, and the constant H0 is the Hubble constant. The velocity is easy to measure: the light of a receding galaxy is shifted towards longer wavelengths, and this redshift can be seen directly in its spectrum. The distance is much harder to measure, and that is what the distance ladder is for. Once H0 is known, the law can also be used the other way round: the redshift gives the distance of any galaxy. H0 also sets the scale of the Universe: how far away the galaxies are and how old the Universe is. The relation was found by Georges Lemaître (1927) and Edwin Hubble (1929); since 2018 the International Astronomical Union has recommended calling it the Hubble–Lemaître law. In parts 3 and 4 of the exercise you measure H0 yourself.

Mida joonis näitabWhat the drawings show

  • Iga keemiline element neelab ja kiirgab valgust ainult oma lainepikkustel. Galaktika spektris on tähtede neeldumisjooned (kaltsium, magneesium, naatrium) ja gaasi kiirgusjooned (vesinik, hapnik, lämmastik). Eemalduva galaktika spektris on kõik jooned nihkunud sama suhte võrra punase poole. See suhe on punanihe z = Δλ / λ.
  • Väikese z korral on kiirus v = cz: punanihe 0.01 tähendab 3000 km/s. Kiiruse mõõtmiseks piisab spektrist, kaugust pole vaja teada. Nii on saadud ka harjutuse 3. ja 4. osa kiirused.
  • Kaugete galaktikate punanihe pole Doppleri nihe läbi ruumi liikumisest. Galaktikatevaheline ruum paisub valguse teekonna ajal ja valguslained venivad koos sellega (vaade Venitus): 1 + z näitab, mitu korda on Universum valguse teekonna ajal paisunud.
  • Lähedal annavad mõlemad vaated samad arvud. Alates z ≈ 0.1 erinevad v = cz, erirelatiivsusteooria Doppleri valem ja tegelik eemaldumiskiirus märgatavalt. Tegelik kiirus ületab valguse kiiruse z ≈ 1.5 juures. See ei riku relatiivsusteooriat: galaktika ei liigu läbi ruumi kiiremini kui valgus, vaid ruum paisub.
  • Lähedased galaktikad, näiteks Andromeeda, võivad olla ka sininihkega. Nende pekuliaarne kiirus on suurem kui paisumine (vt Paisumine).
  • Every chemical element absorbs and emits light only at its own wavelengths. A galaxy spectrum has absorption lines of its stars (calcium, magnesium, sodium) and emission lines of its gas (hydrogen, oxygen, nitrogen). In the spectrum of a receding galaxy, all lines are shifted towards the red by the same ratio. This ratio is the redshift z = Δλ / λ.
  • For a small z, the velocity is v = cz: a redshift of 0.01 means 3000 km/s. Measuring the velocity takes only a spectrum; the distance need not be known. This is also how the velocities of parts 3 and 4 of the exercise were found.
  • The redshift of distant galaxies is not a Doppler shift from motion through space. The space between the galaxies expands while the light travels, and the light waves stretch with it (the Stretch view): 1 + z tells how many times the Universe has expanded during the light's journey.
  • Nearby, both views give the same numbers. From z ≈ 0.1 on, v = cz, the Doppler formula of special relativity and the true recession velocity differ noticeably. The true velocity exceeds the speed of light at z ≈ 1.5. This does not break relativity: the galaxy does not move through space faster than light; space itself expands.
  • Nearby galaxies, such as Andromeda, can even be blueshifted. Their peculiar velocity is larger than the expansion (see Expansion).
  • Kui Universum paisub ühtlaselt, kasvab iga kaugus sama ajaga sama suhte võrra. Siis eemaldub kaks korda kaugem galaktika kaks korda kiiremini, ja nii iga galaktika vaatepunktist. Hubble'i seadus tuleneb paisumisest endast ja Universumil pole keset: klõpsa mõnel teisel galaktikal ja vaata, et sealt paistab kõik samamoodi.
  • Galaktikad liiguvad ka ise, mõnisada km/s, sest nad langevad oma naabrite poole. See on pekuliaarne kiirus. Lähedal on see suur osa kiirusest, kaugel väike. Seepärast hajub harjutuse 3. osa palju rohkem kui 4. osa (vt supernoovade lehel Väike hajuvus).
  • Ka meie liigume: Maa ümber Päikese, Päike ümber Linnutee keskme ja kogu Kohalik galaktikate grupp umbes 620 km/s Hydra–Kentauri superparvede suunas. Seda liikumist näeme kosmilise taustkiirguse dipoolina: ühel pool taevast on kiirgus pisut soojem kui teisel pool.
  • Seepärast viiakse Maal mõõdetud punanihe kõigepealt taustkiirguse süsteemi (zCMB) ja parandatakse siis iga galaktika pekuliaarse kiiruse jaoks, mis arvutatakse tema ümbruse aine jaotusest (zHD). Harjutus kasutab zHD-d. Parandus eemaldab ainult osa: umbes 250 km/s jääb.
  • Hubble'i enda galaktikad olid ainult kuni 2 Mpc kaugusel ja nende kiirused kuni 1000 km/s, kus pekuliaarsed kiirused on veel suured. Kaugused olid umbes 7 korda liiga väikesed: Hubble ei teadnud, et tsefeiide on kahte tüüpi (Walter Baade, 1952), ja kaugemates galaktikates pidas ta heledaid gaasipilvi üksikuteks tähtedeks. Sirge oli siiski olemas. Kaks aastat varem oli Georges Lemaître sama seose leidnud ja selle Universumi paisumisega seletanud.
  • If the Universe expands uniformly, every distance grows by the same ratio in the same time. Then a galaxy twice as far away recedes twice as fast, and this holds from the point of view of every galaxy. The Hubble law follows from the expansion itself, and the Universe has no centre: click another galaxy and see that everything looks the same from there.
  • Galaxies also move on their own, by a few hundred km/s, as they fall towards their neighbours. This is the peculiar velocity. Nearby it is a large part of the velocity, far away a small one. This is why part 3 of the exercise scatters much more than part 4 (see Small scatter on the supernovae page).
  • We move too: the Earth around the Sun, the Sun around the centre of the Milky Way, and the whole Local Group of galaxies at about 620 km/s towards the Hydra–Centaurus superclusters. We see this motion as the dipole of the cosmic microwave background: on one side of the sky the radiation is slightly warmer than on the other.
  • This is why a redshift measured on the Earth is first converted to the frame of the microwave background (zCMB) and then corrected for the peculiar velocity of each galaxy, which is computed from the distribution of matter around it (zHD). The exercise uses zHD. The correction removes only part of it: about 250 km/s remains.
  • Hubble's own galaxies were only up to 2 Mpc away, with velocities up to 1000 km/s, where peculiar velocities are still large. His distances were about 7 times too small: Hubble did not know that there are two types of Cepheids (Walter Baade, 1952), and in the more distant galaxies he took bright gas clouds for single stars. Still, the straight line was there. Two years earlier, Georges Lemaître had found the same relation and explained it by the expansion of the Universe.
  • Kaugest supernoovast tulnud valgus on olnud teel kaua ja Universum on vahepeal paisunud. Siis pole kaugus ja punanihe enam võrdelised ning erinevus sõltub sellest, kas paisumine aeglustub või kiireneb.
  • Harjutuse 4. osa supernoovade jaoks on erinevus väike, umbes 3 %. Tegur (1 + 0.775 z) eemaldab selle: 0.775 = (1 − q0) / 2, kus aeglustusparameeter q0 = −0.55 on leitud kaugematest supernoovadest.
  • q0 < 0 tähendab, et paisumine kiireneb: kauged supernoovad on nõrgemad, kui oleks Universumis, kus on ainult aine. Selle leidsid 1998. aastal Ia tüüpi supernoovadega kaks rühma, ja Saul Perlmutter, Brian Schmidt ja Adam Riess said 2011. aastal Nobeli füüsikaauhinna. Põhjus on tume energia, umbes 70 % Universumi sisust.
  • Vahe on suurim z ≈ 0.5 ja suurema punanihke juures väheneb uuesti: siis oli Universum noorem, aine tihedam ja paisumine aeglustus veel. Tume energia hakkas domineerima alles umbes 5 miljardit aastat tagasi.
  • Harjutuse 4. osa lõpeb z = 0.06 juures, nii et H0 peaaegu ei sõltu mudelist: parandus on seal väike ja hästi teada. Lähemal segavad pekuliaarsed kiirused, kaugemal mudel.
  • Light from a distant supernova has been travelling for a long time, and the Universe has expanded meanwhile. Then distance and redshift are no longer proportional, and the difference depends on whether the expansion slows down or speeds up.
  • For the supernovae of part 4 of the exercise the difference is small, about 3 %. The factor (1 + 0.775 z) removes it: 0.775 = (1 − q0) / 2, where the deceleration parameter q0 = −0.55 has been found from more distant supernovae.
  • q0 < 0 means that the expansion is accelerating: distant supernovae are fainter than they would be in a Universe with only matter. Two teams found this with type Ia supernovae in 1998, and Saul Perlmutter, Brian Schmidt and Adam Riess received the 2011 Nobel Prize in Physics. The cause is dark energy, about 70 % of the content of the Universe.
  • The difference is largest at z ≈ 0.5 and shrinks again at higher redshifts: the Universe was younger then, matter was denser, and the expansion was still slowing down. Dark energy began to dominate only about 5 billion years ago.
  • Part 4 of the exercise ends at z = 0.06, so H0 hardly depends on the model: the correction there is small and well known. Closer in, peculiar velocities get in the way; farther out, the model.
  • H0 ütleb, kui kiiresti kaugused praegu kasvavad: 70 km/s/Mpc juures 1 % 140 miljoni aastaga. Kui kasvukiirus oleks alati olnud sama, oleksid kõik kaugused olnud null 1/H0 tagasi, H0 = 70 korral 14 miljardit aastat. See on Hubble'i aeg, mida harjutuse 3. ja 4. osa näitavad H0 kõrval.
  • Paisumine pole olnud ühtlane. Varem pidurdas seda aine gravitatsioon, nii et paisumine oli kiirem ja Universum on noorem: ainult ainega Universumis 2/3 Hubble'i ajast. Nüüd kiirendab paisumist tume energia. Need kaks peaaegu tasakaalustavad teineteist, nii et tegelik vanus, 13.8 miljardit aastat, on 1/H0 lähedal (0.95 / H0). See on meie ajastu juhus.
  • Vanus kontrollib H0-i: Universum peab olema vanem kui tema vanimad tähed, kerasparvede tähed, mille vanus on 12–13.5 miljardit aastat. Suurem H0 tähendab nooremat Universumit. Hubble'i 1929. aasta väärtus andis vanuseks 2 miljardit aastat, vähem kui Maa vanus 4.5 miljardit aastat. See oli aastakümneid probleem.
  • Harjutuse 4. osas näed oma H0 kõrval ka oma Hubble'i aega.
  • H0 tells how fast distances grow now: at 70 km/s/Mpc, by 1 % in 140 million years. If the rate of growth had always been the same, all distances would have been zero 1/H0 ago, 14 billion years for H0 = 70. This is the Hubble time, which parts 3 and 4 of the exercise show next to H0.
  • The expansion has not been uniform. Earlier, the gravity of matter braked it, so the expansion was faster and the Universe is younger: 2/3 of the Hubble time in a Universe with only matter. Now dark energy accelerates the expansion. The two nearly balance each other, so the true age, 13.8 billion years, is close to 1/H0 (0.95 / H0). This is a coincidence of our epoch.
  • The age is a check on H0: the Universe must be older than its oldest stars, the stars of globular clusters, which are 12–13.5 billion years old. A larger H0 means a younger Universe. Hubble's 1929 value gave an age of 2 billion years, less than the age of the Earth, 4.5 billion years. This was a problem for decades.
  • In part 4 of the exercise you also see your Hubble time next to your H0.
  • Kauguste redel mõõdab H0 praegu, samm-sammult, nagu selles harjutuses. Taustkiirgus annab H0, mida kosmoloogia standardmudel ennustab 380 000 aasta vanuse Universumi järgi. Need peaksid kokku langema, kuid erinevad umbes 8 %.
  • Mõlema viga on nüüd umbes 1 %, nii et erinevus, umbes 5σ, pole juhuslik. See on kas tundmatu süstemaatiline viga ühes meetodis või märk, et standardmudelist puudub midagi, näiteks varane tume energia.
  • Pinge kaotamiseks peaks redelis olema kuskil 0.17 tähesuuruse viga (vaade Redeli vead). Seda on otsinud paljud rühmad. James Webbi kosmoseteleskoop on tsefeiidid uuesti mõõtnud ja leidnud samad kaugused; teised standardküünlad, punaste hiidude haru tipp (TRGB) ja J-riba asümptootilise haru tähed (JAGB), annavad väärtusi kahe vahel.
  • Varase Universumi meetodid (taustkiirgus ja barüonide akustilised võnked, BAO) annavad 66–69, kohalikud 70–74. Meetodid, mis ei kasuta kumbagi, gravitatsiooniläätsed ja gravitatsioonilained, pole veel küllalt täpsed, et otsustada.
  • Harjutuse 4. osas võrdled oma tulemust ise Plancki väärtusega. Avaldatud perioodi-heleduse seosega annab harjutus 3. osas H0 ≈ 67.2 ja 4. osas 70.0 kalibraatorite MB-ga või 72.4 SH0ES-i MB-ga.
  • The distance ladder measures H0 today, step by step, as in this exercise. The microwave background gives the H0 that the standard model of cosmology predicts from the Universe at the age of 380,000 years. The two should agree, but they differ by about 8 %.
  • Both errors are now about 1 %, so the difference, about 5σ, is not chance. It is either an unknown systematic error in one of the methods or a sign that something is missing from the standard model, such as early dark energy.
  • Removing the tension would need an error of 0.17 magnitudes somewhere in the ladder (the Ladder errors view). Many teams have looked for one. The James Webb Space Telescope has measured the Cepheids again and found the same distances; other standard candles, the tip of the red giant branch (TRGB) and J-region asymptotic giant branch stars (JAGB), give values in between.
  • Methods of the early Universe (the microwave background and baryon acoustic oscillations, BAO) give 66–69, local ones 70–74. Methods that use neither, gravitational lenses and gravitational waves, are not yet precise enough to decide.
  • In part 4 of the exercise you compare your result with the Planck value yourself. With the published period–luminosity relation, the exercise gives H0 ≈ 67.2 in part 3, and in part 4 70.0 with the MB of the calibrators or 72.4 with the MB of SH0ES.
